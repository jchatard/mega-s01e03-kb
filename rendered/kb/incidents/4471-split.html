  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field but reads the value day first. People type what the hint says, and the form flips it. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not fix the hint. #4471’s own table marks it “no ticket of its own”.
